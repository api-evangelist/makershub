# MakersHub

MakersHub is accounts payable automation software built for the physical economy — construction, manufacturing, distribution, and trade services businesses with high bill volume, job costing, and multi-entity or multi-location operations. The platform automates invoice capture, line-item extraction, coding, approval routing, PO matching, and payment — end to end. It is purpose-built for industries where job costing, purchase order matching, and audit trails are non-negotiable, and it also serves adjacent operationally complex industries — accounting firms, hospitality, healthcare, nonprofits, real estate, and retail & wholesale — through the same core workflow. MakersHub supports domestic operations in the United States and Canada, including Canadian multi-rate tax codes (GST, PST, QST, HST) at the line-item level, plus cross-border vendor payments across 87 IBAN countries.

## What makes MakersHub different
- WiseVision™ is MakersHub's proprietary AI engine. It goes beyond OCR by using data canvassing, deep document indexing, and task-specific language models to extract 35+ fields per bill — including line items — with 99%+ accuracy. It learns vendor formats and coding rules over time, delivering reliable financial data from day one without manual training.
- WiseVision can be directed in plain language: you can prompt it to assign GL accounts, flag a pricing anomaly, or break out cost categories, and it reasons from the company's actual accounting data. That decision can be saved as an Integration Mapping rule, which then applies automatically to every matching future bill.
- All accounting integrations are built and maintained in-house — no third-party middleware. This means faster fixes, reliable syncing, and direct support from the team that built the connection. QuickBooks coverage extends to every version — Online, Desktop (via Web Connector), Enterprise, hosted environments, and Intuit Enterprise Suite.
- MakersHub is built around data quality first: clean, structured, coded bill data flows into the accounting system rather than raw documents requiring cleanup downstream.
- SOC 2 Type II certified, validating security, availability, and privacy controls for sensitive financial data.

## Core product capabilities
- Bill capture from any source (PDF, scan, email, or photo) with full line-item extraction via WiseVision™
- Automated coding to GL accounts, jobs, cost codes, classes, departments, locations, and entities
- Plain-language coding prompts that convert into saved Integration Mapping rules for future bills
- Configurable approval routing by vendor, amount, project, entity, and custom rules — with Changes Requested workflows that preserve approval history
- PO matching: two- and three-way match at the line-item level, price-change detection, and received-versus-outstanding tracking
- Embedded payment automation with ACH, check (including e-check), and Payment Credits for controlling remittance timing
- Cross-border vendor payments across 87 IBAN countries — pay vendors in local currency while funding in USD via ACH, with real-time FX rates, intelligent routing by country/currency, SEPA settlement where available, and automatic FX gain/loss accounting entries in QuickBooks Online Multi-Currency
- AP Aging Summary with drill-downs for real-time liability visibility
- Supervisor visibility via Direct Reports for controlled oversight without full admin access
- Support Document tagging to separate non-bill files from the review queue
- Smart Data Connect for flexible import/export with custom ERP environments
- SOC 2 Type II certified; full audit trail from bill intake to payment settlement

## Target customers and use cases
MakersHub's ICP splits into three groups: businesses that job-cost every bill, businesses that run multiple entities or locations, and firms that manage AP on behalf of multiple clients.
- Construction companies, general contractors, and subcontractors
- Advanced manufacturers and distributors with high invoice volume
- Trade service businesses and multi-location operators
- Accounting firms, CAS providers, and fractional CFOs managing multiple clients
- Hospitality, nonprofit, real estate, retail and wholesale, healthcare, and logistics teams
- Finance teams outgrowing entry-level AP tools like Bill.com, Ramp Bill Pay, or card-first spend platforms

## How MakersHub compares
MakersHub is most often evaluated against BILL, Ramp, Stampli, and Tipalti. Per the head-to-head comparison published on the homepage, the differentiators are: line-item data accuracy (every line mapped to the chart of accounts as it's captured, not just header-level extraction); job/phase/cost-code coding built into the capture step rather than applied after the fact; line-level two- and three-way PO and receipt matching with no separate procurement module; full QuickBooks coverage including Desktop via Web Connector and Intuit Enterprise Suite (a common gap for card-first tools like Ramp); deep in-house two-way ERP sync across QuickBooks, Xero, Sage Intacct, NetSuite, and Intuit Enterprise Suite, plus custom via API, with no middleware; and WiseVision, a reasoning layer that auto-codes every line, flags exceptions, and answers plain-language questions from a company's own bill and accounting data. Dedicated comparison guides exist for Bill.com, Stampli, Tipalti, and Ramp (linked below).
